SIGEA

The Standard in Detection Engineering

Sigea builds open-source tools for threat informed detection engineering. We start from what adversaries actually do, work back to the rules that would catch them, and then keep checking that those rules still can.

Transparency

Open source and auditable. Every score, finding and coverage decision shows its working, so you can explain it to an auditor, or to yourself at 3am.

Alignment

Defences mapped to MITRE ATT&CK®: baselines built from the groups, software and campaigns that target you, not from a generic checklist.

Automation

Detection engineering as a lifecycle: find the gap, build the rule, deploy it, watch it work. The tooling does the legwork; a person makes every decision.

Why SIGEA?

Read it backwards.

SIGEA is AEGIS spelled in reverse. In Greek myth the aegis was the shield carried by Zeus and by Athena, goddess of wisdom and strategic warfare. It bore the Gorgon's head, which is the face in our logo, and it is why we still say something happens "under the aegis" of someone: under their protection.

Athena never won by charging in. That was Ares' department. She won by knowing what was coming and being ready for it, which is as good a description of detection engineering as we have found.

And the backwards spelling? That is how threat informed detection works: start from the adversary's behaviour and work back to the rule that catches it.

Meet TIDE

TIDE (Threat Informed Detection Engine) is our open-source platform for the whole detection lifecycle: from the threat actors that matter to you, to the rules in your SIEM, to proof that those rules can still fire. It runs air-gapped, keeps each client apart, and ships with MITRE ATT&CK, SigmaHQ and Elastic's detection rules built in.

The latest release teaches Rule Monitoring to notice when the data behind a rule goes quiet, arrives late or never fills the fields it matches, and shows how much of your coverage is effective.
