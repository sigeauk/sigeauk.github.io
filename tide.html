TIDE

Threat Informed Detection Engineering

TIDE answers the critical question: "If an adversary attacked today, would your rules actually catch them?" A standalone, containerized platform for auditing detection health and mapping live threat intelligence to defensive coverage.

Core Platform Capabilities

TIDE bridges the gap between raw threat intelligence and validated SIEM operations through a Human-in-the-Loop workflow.

Rule Health Scoring

Automated composite scoring (0–100) based on Quality (logic & field mapping) and Metadata completeness.

Adversary Mapping

Visual intersection of posture vs. behavior. Identify coverage gaps (Red) and Defense-in-Depth (Blue) against specific threat actors.

Automated Reporting

Generate CISO-ready Executive Summaries and Technical Audit PDFs server-side via WeasyPrint—no external dependencies required.

Sigma Lifecycle

Browse, convert, and stage SigmaHQ rules. Deploy verified logic directly to your SIEM staging environment with one click.

Asset Assurance

Link CVEs to MITRE techniques and active detection rules to calculate RAG (Red/Amber/Green) status across your inventory.

Air-Gapped by Design

Built for secure environments. Sigma rules and MITRE ATT&CK bundles are shipped inside the image for zero-dependency operation.

View Technical Architecture & Stack

Three-Tier Architecture

Designed for stability and persistence in enterprise environments.

Edge: Nginx

Handles SSL termination and secure reverse proxying.

Core: FastAPI

Asynchronous REST API powering the HTMX dynamic frontend and business logic.

Data: DuckDB

Embedded OLAP database for high-performance rule caching and analytics without external DB overhead.

Data Persistence

TIDE maintains critical state across container updates via volume mounts at /app/data/:

  • tide.duckdb: Central rule cache and coverage metrics.
  • checkedRule.json: Manual analyst validation records.
  • triggers/: Background synchronization state.
