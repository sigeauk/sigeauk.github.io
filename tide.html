TIDE

Threat Informed Detection Engineering

TIDE answers the critical question: "If an adversary attacked today, would your rules actually catch them?" A standalone, containerized platform for auditing detection health and mapping live threat intelligence to defensive coverage.

Core Platform Capabilities

TIDE bridges the gap between raw threat intelligence and validated SIEM operations.

Rule Health Scoring

Automated composite scoring (0–100) based on Quality (logic & field mapping) and Metadata completeness.

Adversary Mapping

Visual intersection of posture vs. behavior. Identify coverage gaps (Red) and Defense-in-Depth (Blue) against specific threat actors.

Automated Reporting

Generate CISO-ready Executive Summaries and Technical Audit PDFs server-side—no external dependencies required.

Sigma Lifecycle

Browse, convert, and stage SigmaHQ rules. Deploy verified logic directly to your SIEM staging environment with one click.

Asset Assurance

Link CVEs to MITRE techniques and active detection rules to calculate RAG (Red/Amber/Green) status across your inventory.

Air-Gapped by Design

Built for secure environments. Sigma rules and MITRE ATT&CK bundles are shipped inside the image for zero-dependency operation.

View Technical Architecture & Data Bundling

Edge: Nginx

Reverse proxy, SSL termination, and static asset serving for secure access.

Core: FastAPI

REST API and HTMX partial endpoints handling the business logic and lifecycle workflow.

Data: DuckDB

High-performance embedded analytics database—zero external database server required.

The Air-Gap Strategy

Unlike standard tools that query external APIs, TIDE bundles threat context at build-time to ensure 100% functionality in isolated networks.
