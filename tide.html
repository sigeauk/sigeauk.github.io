TIDE

Threat Informed Detection Engine

If an adversary attacked today, would your rules actually catch them? TIDE answers that question per system you defend: which threats your detection rules cover, which of those rules can still work on the data you collect, and who accepted every gap that is left.

Latest release 6.4.0  Open source, air-gapped, and two Docker containers to run.

What TIDE does

Rule Health

Every rule from your SIEMs in one place, scored as a percentage on the checks you choose and weight per client: field mappings checked against your real indexes, investigation guides, MITRE tags, search time and more. Filter, validate, and see each rule's score over time.

Rule Monitoring

Kibana says when a rule fails. TIDE says when it quietly can't work:

  • missing indexes and fields, failed runs, blind windows
  • data gone quiet, fields never filled, events arriving late
  • noisy, silent and spiking rules, slow searches
  • exception sprawl, duplicates, upstream drift

Effective coverage

Systems and baselines show what each environment should detect and what covers it. Effective coverage leaves out rules that can't work right now, so a green tick means a rule that can still fire. Known gaps carry a reason, an owner and a review date.

Threat informed baselines

Build a baseline from any ATT&CK group, software or campaign, or from your OpenCTI's intrusion sets, then map rules to each technique. The heatmap and Threat Landscape show how much of each actor's tradecraft your enabled rules carry.

Sigma and Elastic libraries

SigmaHQ and Elastic's detection rules are built in. Filter to what you need, convert and deploy in a click, or make a baseline from the rules you filtered. TIDE tracks where each rule came from and shows you upstream updates to review and merge.

The rule lifecycle

Create and edit query, EQL, ES|QL, threshold, new terms and indicator match rules. Duplicate, move or copy them between SIEM destinations, compare two rules section by section, and see every change in a rule's history, whoever made it and wherever.

Reports and GitLab

Executive and technical system and threat reports in PDF or Markdown, with the health of the rules behind the figures. Back up every rule to GitLab, and raise Rule Monitoring's findings as GitLab issues for the person who fixes them.

Built for secure networks

No internet needed once installed: MITRE ATT&CK, NIST 800-53, SigmaHQ, Elastic's rules and the CISA KEV catalogue ship inside the image. Each client gets its own database, users and roles, with Keycloak single sign-on and an out-of-hours maintenance window.
